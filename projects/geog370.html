03 / FINAL RISK ASSESSMENT

Interpreting the Risk Index

The final index combines food access hazards and socioeconomic vulnerability to identify counties with potentially greater barriers to food access among older adults.

The index represents relative risk based on selected county-level indicators. It should not be interpreted as a direct measurement of individual food insecurity.
