PROJECT 01 / GIS INFOGRAPHIC

Open Space in the Tri-Metro Region

Description

This project examines the distribution of parkland across Clackamas, Multnomah, and Washington counties in the Portland Tri-Metro region.

Using ArcGIS Pro, I combined spatial analysis, thematic mapping, and statistical visualization to communicate patterns of open space to a general audience. I calculated park acreage and the percentage of county land designated as parks, then presented the results through a map, two bar charts, and key statistics.

The analysis identified Clackamas County as having the largest amount of parkland, with approximately 79,000 acres, representing about 6.5% of its area.
