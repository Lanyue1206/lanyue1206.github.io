About Me

I’m Lanyue Zhang, an undergraduate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill double majoring in Biology and Geospatial Data Science, with a minor in Data Science. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

In spring 2025, I studied abroad at the University of St Andrews in Scotland, where I took courses in ecology, animal behaviour, vertebrate zoology, and geography.

My research uses airborne LiDAR to study changes in forest structure and aboveground biomass. At UNC, I also tutor students in GIS at the Learning Center and mentor first-generation, first-year students through HeelGuide.
